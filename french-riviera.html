Trip notes

Intro

Next September we're taking the crew to the Côte d'Azur. We'll be chartering a 42' catamaran out of Saint-Mandrier, near Toulon, for a week of Mediterranean sailing — seaside towns, great food, anchorages in the national parks, and some scuba diving along the way. We're weighing two route options: heading east toward Saint-Tropez, or west toward Marseille and the calanques. Either way, expect a mix of lively port towns and quiet nights on the hook.

Itinerary

The route is still being finalized — we're deciding between an eastern loop toward Saint-Tropez and a western loop toward Marseille. The full day-by-day itinerary with distances and sailing times will be posted here once it's locked in.

Our Yacht

We're chartering a 42-foot catamaran through PPR Charters out of Saint-Mandrier. Photos and the cabin layout will be posted here once the boat is confirmed.

What to Pack

The full packing list will be posted here closer to the trip. The usual rules apply: soft luggage only, swim and snorkel gear, a jacket for evenings on the water, and reef-safe everything. The FAQ covers the rest.

Sailing Safety
